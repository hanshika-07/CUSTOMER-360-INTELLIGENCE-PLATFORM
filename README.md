

\# Customer 360 Intelligence Platform



\## Project Overview



The Customer 360 Intelligence Platform is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to bring together customer, sales, marketing, product usage, payment, and customer support data to generate meaningful business insights.



The project uses Python for data generation, cleaning, and analysis, SQL for querying data, and Power BI for interactive dashboards and KPI reporting.



\## Objectives



\- Analyze customer profiles and customer segments.

\- Understand sales performance and revenue trends.

\- Evaluate marketing spend and conversion performance.

\- Analyze product usage and customer engagement.

\- Monitor payment statuses and payment success rates.

\- Evaluate customer support tickets and service performance.

\- Present insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.



\## Tools and Technologies



\- \*\*Python:\*\* Data generation and preprocessing

\- \*\*Pandas:\*\* Data manipulation and cleaning

\- \*\*NumPy:\*\* Numerical calculations and conditional transformations

\- \*\*SQL:\*\* Data querying and analysis

\- \*\*Power BI:\*\* Interactive dashboards and visualization

\- \*\*DAX:\*\* Business measures and KPI calculations

\- \*\*Matplotlib:\*\* Statistical and analytical visualizations
